TWO DISCOUNTS ON ONE CLOUD CONTRACT, AND WHAT PEDRO ACTUALLY OWES

In this article ❧ Cloud contracts rarely carry one price reduction. A commitment lowers the list price, then a negotiated agreement lowers what remains, and each concession lands on a base the previous one already moved. This problem shows why buyers who add their discounts together overstate the savings and understate the invoice.

The Problem

Pedro signed a cloud commitment that cut the list price of an annual reserved compute plan by 25%. The provider then applied a 20% enterprise agreement discount to all final prices.

How much did Pedro pay for a plan listed at $825,000?

  • a) 330,000
  • b) 742,500
  • c) 701,250
  • d) 495,000
Click to Reveal the Answer

Correct Answer, d) 495,000 ✅

Solution

Each discount is a multiplication by the fraction of price that survives it, so the commitment leaves 75% and the agreement leaves 80% of that. Multiplying 825,000 by 0.75 and then by 0.80 gives 495,000.

Two reductions of 25% and 20% do not remove 45% of the list price, they remove 40%.

Step by Step Breakdown

The following sequence shows how the final amount is derived:

  1. Fraction retained after the commitment discount, 100% − 25% = 75%, or 0.75
  2. Price after the first discount, 825,000 × 0.75 = 618,750
  3. Fraction retained after the agreement discount, 100% − 20% = 80%, or 0.80
  4. Price after the second discount, 618,750 × 0.80 = 495,000
  5. Combined factor check, 0.75 × 0.80 = 0.60, and 825,000 × 0.60 = 495,000
  6. Effective discount, 1 − 0.60 = 0.40, so 40% off list
  7. Conclusion, Pedro pays 495,000 for the plan

Three Routes to the Same Answer

A buyer sitting across the table has no spreadsheet open. There are three ways to reach the figure, ordered from fastest to slowest.

Fastest, by estimation

Frame the task as 80% of 75% of the price, so 0.80(0.75x) where x is 825,000.

  • 75% of 825 is close to 8 × 8, so about 620
  • 80% of 620 is close to 8 × 6, so about 480
  • The estimate lands near 480,000, which points to d

This route works only when no two options sit close together. Here the nearest rival, 495,000, is the answer itself, so the estimate is safe.

Elegant, with fractions

  • 80% is 4/5 of something
  • 75% is 3/4 of something
  • 4/5 of 3/4 of 5/5 is 60/100
  • 0.60 of 825,000 is 495,000

Longest, step by step

  • 25% is 1/4
  • 20% is 1/5
  • 3/4 of 825,000 is 618,750
  • 4/5 of 618,750 is 495,000

The move worth keeping, instead of computing 1/4 and 1/5 and subtracting each from the price, subtract 1/4 from 4/4 to get 3/4 and 1/5 from 5/5 to get 4/5, then take those shares of the original price. Half the operations.

Historical Background

Assessment in hundredths came out of Roman fiscal administration, where levies on sales and inheritances were fixed as a share of a hundred. The centesima rerum venalium, a 1% duty charged on goods sold at auction, is one recorded case. Merchant manuals in Renaissance Italy later worked out chained reductions in detail, because a trader moving goods through several ports faced a customs charge, a broker fee, and a currency spread in sequence, and each one applied to what the previous step had left.
