800 TICKETS, ONE SCALING RULE, FOUR CLEAN WEEKS

In this article ❧ Capacity targets rarely stay put. When a team raises the bar on a daily task, planners need to project what that new pace produces across a stretch of working days before they commit to it. This problem shows how a short chain of multiplications turns a daily target into a monthly total, and how Python can hold that logic as a reusable tool.

The Problem

To hit her new quarterly target, a support agent must handle 4 times more chat tickets and email tickets each day. She currently handles 10 chat tickets and 5 email tickets per day, and she works 5 days a week.

Under the new target, how many chat tickets will she handle over 4 weeks?

  • a) 1,200
  • b) 80
  • c) 39
  • d) 800
Click to Reveal the Answer

Correct Answer: d) 800 ✅

Math Solution

Scale the daily chat load with 4 × 10 = 40, count the working days with 5 × 4 = 20, then multiply the two to reach the total. The email tickets do not enter the chat total.

The new pace is 40 chat tickets per day, so every working day adds 40 to the running count.

Step-by-Step Breakdown

The following sequence shows how the final amount is derived:

  1. Scale to target: 4 × 10 = 40 chat tickets per day
  2. Working days: 5 × 4 = 20 days
  3. Combine: total = 40 × 20
  4. Multiply: 40 × 20 = 800
  5. Result: 800 chat tickets over 4 weeks

Roadmap

Steps 1 and 2 run in parallel, then merge at step 3. The path ends at 800.

Proof

Mode: Compute.

Setup. Let T be the total chat tickets over 4 weeks, a positive integer. The new daily chat load is the scale factor 4 times the current rate 10. Working days are 5 per week across 4 weeks. Email tickets at 5 per day do not enter T. So T equals the quantity 4 times 10, times the quantity 5 times 4.

Solution

  1. Associativity of multiplication. For any a, b, c, the product a times b times c does not depend on grouping. The new daily chat load is 40. Group the scale factor 4 with the base rate 10, so 4 times 10 equals 40.
  2. Associativity of multiplication. As in step 1. The total working days are 20. Group days per week 5 with weeks 4, so 5 times 4 equals 20.
  3. Substitution. Equals may replace equals. T equals 40 times 20. Builds on lines 1 and 2, replacing each grouped quantity with its value.
  4. Associativity of multiplication. As in step 1. 40 times 20 equals 800. Regroup as 40 times 2 times 10, giving 80 times 10, which is 800. Builds on line 3.
  5. Transitivity of equality. If a equals b and b equals c then a equals c. Therefore T equals 800. Builds on lines 3 and 4.

Verification. Divide 800 by the 20 working days. The result is 40 per day, which equals 4 times 10, the new daily load. Both sides match.

Answer. The agent handles 800 chat tickets over 4 weeks.
